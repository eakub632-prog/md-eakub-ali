About

I am Md. Eakub Ali, a graduate of Persian Language and Literature from the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh. I study classical and modern Persian and Bengali literature, and I enjoy working with old manuscripts.

Alongside my studies I have served as an elected hall union representative, a club executive and a blood-donation volunteer. I also create digital content: photography, video editing and storytelling for the screen. I work in Bangla, Persian and English.
